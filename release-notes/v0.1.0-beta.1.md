# Forever Achievements — First Beta (v0.1.0-beta.1)

Character and account achievements for **World of Warcraft: Forever**: 422 achievements, a window to browse them,
toasts, a tracker, statistics and an account view. Open it with the minimap button or `/fach`.

> ⚠️ **Beta:** everything is covered by automated tests, but **nothing has been verified in game yet**. Every client API the addon reads is assumed, not measured, and the Forever launch is on 2026-11-05. Expect some achievements not to be detected until the first real measurements. Please report anything odd.

## ✨ Features

### Catalog (422 achievements)
- **General** (40): level, wealth, time played, collections, emotes and miscellaneous
- **Quests** (19) and **Exploration** (44)
- **Player vs. Player** (40): honor and battlegrounds
- **Dungeons and raids** (100): 34 five-player and 66 raid achievements
- **Professions** (52): 40 primary and 12 secondary
- **Reputation** (97): factions, capitals, world and PvP
- **Feats of Strength** (14) and **Account** (16)

### Detection
- A retroactive scan at login grants, silently, what your character already meets: level, money, time played, quests, exploration, reputation, professions, collections, honorable kills, items owned and gear quality
- Event detectors count, from the moment you install the addon: boss kills, battlegrounds played and won, deaths, emotes aimed at creatures and money looted
- During a boss encounter the addon waits and evaluates when it ends. The combat log is never used

### Window
- Summary, Achievements and Statistics pages, with a **Character / Account** switch
- Category tree with counts, search, *All / Completed / Incomplete* filters and the criteria of each achievement
- **Shift-click** an achievement to track it; a movable tracker shows up to 10 with their progress
- Toasts, chat lines and a sound when you earn one, each with its own switch (gear icon)
- Minimap button: click to open, drag to move, hide it from the settings
- English, and Spanish on `esES` and `esMX` clients

### Account and statistics
- The Account view sums your characters and shows who earned each achievement first (**Earned by …**, or **before the addon** for retroactive grants)
- 16 account achievements: characters at a level, different classes, gold and points across the account
- Per-character statistics: deaths, quests, highest level, emotes, bosses, honorable kills, battlegrounds, money looted, epic items and mounts

### Privacy
- Everything is stored locally in `ForeverAchievementsDB`. The addon sends no addon messages and makes no network calls. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/Fragudev/addon-forever-achievements/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md)

## 🧭 How to use

1. Install it with the CurseForge app or unzip the release into `Interface/AddOns/`, and log in
2. Click the minimap button or type **`/fach`** to open the window
3. Pick a category, search, and click an achievement to see its criteria. **Shift-click** to track it
4. Use the **gear** for toasts, chat and sound, and the **Character / Account** switch to change view
5. `/fach help` lists every command: `/fach track <id>`, `/fach forget <name>` and `/fach debug …`

The full walkthrough is the [user guide](https://github.com/Fragudev/addon-forever-achievements/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md).

## 🐞 Known limitations

- **Not tested in game.** The client APIs are unmeasured; the checks to run at launch are in issue [#2](https://github.com/Fragudev/addon-forever-achievements/issues/2). It is also unknown whether Forever ships its own achievement system
- **Unverified catalog ids** (issue [#3](https://github.com/Fragudev/addon-forever-achievements/issues/3)): some encounter, zone, item and creature ids and icons come from public sources and may not match the Forever client, so some achievements may not complete
- **Not in the catalog yet:** class quests at level 60, the Hydraxian Waterlords and Ahn'Qiraj war effort quests, the Paladin Charger quest, Alterac Mountains exploration, and full clears of Blackfathom Deeps, Dire Maul and Upper Blackrock Spire
- **Past events are not credited:** boss kills, battlegrounds, deaths, emotes and looted money are counted only from the moment the addon is installed
- **No combat log:** the Midnight addon restrictions block it, so anything that would need it cannot be detected
